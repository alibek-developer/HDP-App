## Brand & Style

HDP Academy Math - Telegram WebApp design system. EdTech interface built to dismantle math anxiety. Combining geometric precision with organic warmth, the aesthetic balances modern SaaS rigor with friendly, tactile learning environments.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative yet Inviting:** Serious mathematical rigor presented through calm, non-intimidating visual structures.
- **Focus-Oriented:** Zero decorative distraction; visual interest is generated through crisp typography, elegant data viz, and tactile feedback.
- **Encouraging:** Growth-oriented emerald and mint tones instill confidence, clarity, and positive reinforcement during complex problem-solving.

### Aesthetic Direction
Modern geometric minimalism rooted in clean white surfaces, subtle tonal grouping, balanced emerald accents, and precise geometric typography.
